How to choose a study-and-work destination

Five numbers matter more than rankings.

⏱️

1. Legal work hours

New Zealand (25 h/week), Canada (24 h/week) and Australia (48 h per fortnight) are the most generous during term. The USA restricts F-1 students to on-campus work.

💵

2. Wage vs. rent

High minimum wages (Australia, Ireland, UK) only help if rent is manageable. Use the earnings estimator to compare cover ratios.

🧳

3. Post-study work length

Longer post-study rights (Canada, New Zealand, Australia research, US STEM OPT) give you more time to recover your investment.

🏦

4. Proof of funds

Most visas require proof you can cover tuition and living costs without relying on part-time work. Plan savings first.

🎓

5. Program eligibility

Work rights often depend on the institution (e.g. Canada's DLI list, Ireland's ILEP) and course level. Check before you pay a deposit.

📈

6. Pathway to residence

Some countries reward local work experience with permanent-residence points. Treat this as a bonus, not a guarantee.
