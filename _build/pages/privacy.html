Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

StudyWork ("we", "us") operates this website as an independent information service. For privacy questions, use our contact form.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us through forms: name, email, phone, destination, study preferences, grades, budget and messages.
  • Usage data (with consent): pages viewed, device and approximate location via analytics.
  • Local preferences stored in your browser (theme, saved scholarships, cookie choice). These never leave your device.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare the plan or information you requested.
  • To send newsletters or alerts you subscribed to (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To introduce you to a university, lender, employer or other partner only when you explicitly consent.
  • To improve the site and measure performance.

4. Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-delivery service (FormSubmit). Submitted data is transmitted securely over HTTPS.

5. Cookies & advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), we request consent before non-essential cookies are used.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data with service providers (hosting, form delivery, analytics, advertising) and with partners only with your consent, or where required by law.

7. Retention

We keep enquiry data for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, India's DPDP Act), you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data, and withdraw consent. Contact us through the contact form.

9. Children

StudyWork is intended for users aged 16 and over. Contest entry requires age 18+.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
