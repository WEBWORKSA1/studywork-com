Enter the contest

One entry per person per category. Entries are judged on authenticity (40%), usefulness to future students (40%) and creativity (20%).

  • Free — no purchase necessary
  • Open to current and prospective students aged 18+
  • You keep copyright; you grant StudyWork a licence to feature your entry

Official rules (summary)

Eligibility

Open to individuals aged 18+ who are current or prospective students, where not prohibited by local law. Employees of StudyWork and sponsors, and their immediate families, are not eligible. Void where prohibited.

Entry period

Entries close at the time shown on the countdown (23:59 UTC on 31 December 2026) unless extended by notice on this page.

Judging & winners

A panel appointed by StudyWork judges entries on the published criteria. Winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days, or an alternate winner may be chosen. Decisions are final.

Prizes

Prizes are as published on this page at the close of entries. Prizes are non-transferable, may be paid by bank transfer or an equivalent method, and winners are responsible for any taxes. If a sponsor withdraws, StudyWork may substitute a prize of similar value.

Content & licence

Entries must be original, lawful and must not infringe third-party rights. You keep copyright and grant StudyWork a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to publish your entry with credit. We may reject entries at our discretion.

Privacy

Entry data is used to administer the contest and, if you opt in, to send you the StudyWork brief. See our Privacy Policy.
