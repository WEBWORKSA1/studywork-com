Scholarship FAQs

Do you charge to apply?

No. StudyWork never charges students, and legitimate scholarships never ask for an application "fee" paid to a third party. If anyone asks you to pay to receive a scholarship, treat it as a scam.

Are deadlines listed here?

Deadlines change every cycle, so we link to each official page instead of copying dates that go stale. Many major programs open 9–12 months before the start of study.

Can I list my scholarship?

Yes — universities, foundations and companies can submit or sponsor a listing.
