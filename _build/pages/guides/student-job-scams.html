Student job scams: 10 red flags

By StudyWork Editorial Team· Reviewed {{UPDATED}}· 6 min read

New arrivals are targeted because they're eager to earn, unfamiliar with local norms and worried about their visa. Knowing the patterns is the best protection.

The 10 red flags

  1. You're asked to pay for training, uniforms, registration or "visa processing" before starting.
  2. A cheque arrives and you're asked to deposit it and send part of the money elsewhere.
  3. The job offer comes without an interview, often via messaging apps.
  4. Pay seems too high for simple tasks like "reshipping parcels" or "processing payments".
  5. They ask for your bank login, passport scan or tax number before a written contract.
  6. Cash-only, no payslips — often a sign of illegal employment.
  7. Pressure to exceed your visa hours "just this once".
  8. Callers claiming to be immigration officials demanding immediate payment.
  9. Housing-plus-job bundles that require an upfront deposit before viewing.
  10. Email domains that don't match the company website.

If you've been targeted

  • Stop all contact and don't send more money.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you shared details or deposited a cheque.
  • Report it to your university's international student office and the national fraud reporting service.
  • Keep screenshots and messages as evidence.
Legitimate employers never charge you to work, and government agencies don't demand payment by gift card, crypto or wire transfer.
