Counselling FAQs

Why is it free?

StudyWork is funded by advertising, sponsorships and donations. Some partner institutions or lenders may pay a referral fee only if you choose to be introduced to them — it never changes the price you pay.

Will you apply for my visa?

No. We are not immigration consultants and don't prepare or submit visa applications. We point you to official sources and, where needed, suggest you consult a licensed adviser.

What happens to my data?

We use your details only to prepare your plan and follow up. We never sell your data, and we share it with a partner only with your explicit consent. See our Privacy Policy.
