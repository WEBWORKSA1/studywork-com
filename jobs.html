Student jobs & internships

Legal part-time work, on-campus roles, co-ops and graduate internships — where to look, what pays, and how to stay within your visa rules.

Most common student job categories

🏫

On-campus

Library, labs, IT help desk, student services, research and teaching assistant roles. Often the first (and in the USA, the only) option.

☕

Hospitality & retail

Cafés, restaurants, supermarkets and stores offer flexible shifts that fit around lectures.

💻

Tutoring & digital

Tutoring, content writing, data annotation and social media — check whether remote or freelance work is allowed on your visa.

🏥

Care & support

Healthcare assistant and support-worker roles are in demand in the UK, Australia, Canada and Ireland (training and checks usually required).

📦

Logistics & delivery

Warehouse and seasonal roles pay well during breaks. Gig delivery may count as self-employment — not allowed in some countries.

🧪

Co-op & placements

Paid work terms built into your program (e.g. Canadian co-op, UK placement years) that often don't count toward weekly limits.

🎯

Internships

Summer and graduate internships — the fastest route to a post-study job offer.

🌐

Remote employers

Remote work for overseas employers has complex tax/visa implications. Get advice before accepting.

🚩 Student job scam red flags

Pay-to-work

Any "registration", "training" or "visa processing" fee to start a job.

Cash & cheque tricks

Being asked to deposit a cheque and send money on, or to be paid only in cash "off the books".

Hours beyond your limit

Employers pressuring you to exceed legal hours put your visa at risk, not theirs.
