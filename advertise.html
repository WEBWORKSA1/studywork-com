Our standards

Clearly labelled

All paid placements are marked "Sponsored" or "Advertisement". Editorial guides are never for sale.

Consent-first leads

Students are only introduced to partners after explicit opt-in. We never sell data lists.

Student-safe partners

We decline pay-to-work schemes, unlicensed immigration services and predatory lenders.
