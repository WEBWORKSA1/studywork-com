Frequently asked questions

Is StudyWork free for students?

Yes. Tools, guides, the scholarship finder and counselling requests are free. We are funded by advertising, partner sponsorships and voluntary donations.

Can I really pay for my studies by working part-time?

Usually only partly. At legal term-time limits, part-time income typically covers a share of living costs, not tuition. Use the earnings estimator and funding-gap calculator to see your real numbers.

How current is the visa and work-rule information?

We review rules regularly (last review: September 2026) and every rule links to its official government source. Rules change often, so always confirm on the official page before acting.

Are you an immigration consultant or a government agency?

No. StudyWork is an independent information service. We do not give legal or immigration advice and are not affiliated with any government. For visa applications, use official channels or a licensed adviser.
