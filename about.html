About StudyWork

Studying abroad is one of the biggest financial decisions a family makes. StudyWork exists so students can see the full picture — costs, legal work options, funding and career pathways — before they commit.

🎯

Mission

Make study-and-work planning transparent, data-driven and free for every student, everywhere.

🔍

Editorial policy

Every rule links to an official source. Guides are reviewed on a regular cycle and dated. Sponsored content is always labelled and never changes our guides.

💡

How we're funded

Advertising (including Google AdSense), labelled sponsorships, consent-based partner referrals and donations. Students never pay.

Spotted an error or outdated rule?

Rules change quickly. Tell us and we'll review it within 72 hours.
