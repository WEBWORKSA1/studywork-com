Study online while working full-time

By StudyWork Editorial Team· Reviewed September 2026· 6 min read

Not everyone can move abroad. Online and part-time programs let you upgrade your qualifications while keeping your salary — the other side of "study work".

Choosing a credible program

  • Check accreditation by a recognised national body, and whether the degree is identical to the on-campus version.
  • Look at total cost, not per-credit price, and at completion rates.
  • Prefer programs with flexible deadlines and recorded lectures if you work shifts.
  • Stackable certificates let you earn credentials step by step.

Get your employer to pay

Many employers offer tuition assistance. Make a one-page business case: the skills you'll gain, how they apply to your role, the cost, and a commitment to stay for a set period.

A weekly system that works

  1. Block three 90-minute study sessions on weekdays and one longer weekend session.
  2. Do reading on your commute; save focused work for the blocks.
  3. Plan assessments at the start of term and warn your manager about crunch weeks.
  4. Protect one full rest day every week.
Consistency beats intensity: 8 focused hours a week for a year is more than most people ever study.
