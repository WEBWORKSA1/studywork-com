Post-study work visas compared

By StudyWork Editorial Team· Reviewed September 2026· 8 min read

The right to work after graduation is the single most important driver of study-abroad return on investment. A two-year degree followed by three years of skilled work looks very different financially from a degree followed by an immediate return home. Here's how the main post-study options compare.

Summary

  • Canada — PGWP: generally matches program length, up to 3 years; master's graduates can qualify for up to 3 years. Field-of-study requirements apply to some college programs.
  • United Kingdom — Graduate Route: 2 years for applications made by 31 December 2026; 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027; PhD graduates 3 years.
  • Australia — subclass 485 (Post-Higher Education Work): around 2 years for bachelor's and coursework master's, 3 years for research master's and PhD; age limits apply.
  • USA — OPT: 12 months, plus a 24-month STEM OPT extension for eligible STEM degrees.
  • Germany: up to 18 months to find a job related to your qualification.
  • Ireland — Stamp 1G: 12 months (Level 8), up to 24 months (Level 9+).
  • New Zealand: post-study work visa of up to 3 years depending on qualification.
  • France — APS: 12 months to look for work, renewable in some cases.

Why it matters: a quick ROI example

Suppose a master's program costs 60,000 in total and raises your annual salary from 15,000 to 55,000. If you save 40% of that 40,000 uplift, payback takes about 3.75 years. A 3-year post-study visa covers most of that period abroad; an 18-month visa means more of the payback happens after you return home — often at a lower salary. Model your own numbers with our ROI calculator.

How to make the most of post-study work time

  1. Start job-hunting in your final term, not after graduation. Graduate programs recruit 6–12 months ahead.
  2. Use internships and co-ops during your studies to build local experience.
  3. Target shortage occupations — they tend to have better sponsorship and residence prospects.
  4. Track your deadlines. Most post-study visas must be applied for within a fixed window after completing your course.
  5. Keep your status valid — gaps can make you ineligible.
Rules on eligible programs, age limits and application windows change frequently. Confirm every detail with the official immigration website for your destination.
